# Bank of Scotland: Smart Cost of Living

> **The brief:** Everyday financial decisions involve trade-offs. The cheapest option might take longer, the most convenient might cost more, and the most sustainable choice may not always be the easiest. Create a solution that helps customers navigate these trade-offs and make better decisions about their money and everyday lives. Think creatively about what "better" means.

## The decision we focus on: choosing a home to rent

For most renters, rent is the largest monthly outgoing, and the choice locks them in for a year or more. It's also the decision with the worst information. A listing shows the rent, but not:

- the **council tax** for that band;
- the **energy bill** a poorly insulated flat will run up;
- the **time and cost of getting to work or university**;
- whether the home **works for them** (a step, no lift, a long walk to a stop);
- **condition problems** like damp and mould that lead to costs and health problems later.

Edinburgh declared a housing emergency in November 2023, and a third of Scottish households were in fuel poverty in 2023. A wrong choice here is expensive and hard to undo.

## The trade-offs Haven makes visible

The brief names the trade-offs exactly. These are the ones a renter faces, and how Haven shows each:

| Trade-off | Example | How Haven shows it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cheapest vs total cost** | A low rent in a poorly insulated flat can cost more each month than a slightly higher rent in an efficient one | The **true monthly cost** (rent + council tax + modelled energy) against the renter's budget, not just the rent |
| **Cheaper vs takes longer** | A cheaper flat further out means longer commutes | **Travel time** to the renter's own places and **walking distance** to the nearest stop |
| **Convenience vs cost** | Central flats save time but cost more | The map colours homes by **cost against budget** or **travel time**, so you can see both side by side |
| **Sustainability vs ease** | An efficient home cuts both bills and emissions | **Heating cost risk** from the EPC band, priced at the current Ofgem cap |
| **Price vs fair value** | Is this rent normal for the area? | **Rent context**: where the rent sits among Lothian quartiles, and against the Local Housing Allowance |
| **Price vs condition** | A bargain with hidden damp | **Photo screening** for possible damp and mould before you commit |

## What "better" means in Haven

The brief asks us to think about what "better" means. Haven's answer: **"better" is personal**, so the renter defines it.

- A **student** may weigh rent and travel time to university most.
- A **renter on a tight budget** may weigh the total monthly cost above all.
- A **wheelchair user** may need step-free access and a lift first, with cost second.

The renter profile sets the budget, priorities and access needs. Every rule uses the renter's thresholds, and the map's colour coding follows their priorities. Haven **never collapses this into one score**. It shows the trade-offs and lets the person decide.

## How it maps to the suggested approaches

| Brief suggests | What Haven does |
| --- | --- |
| **AI** | Google Gemini screens photos for possible damp, mould and entrance steps, and can optionally reword the report in plainer language |
| **Optimisation** | Comparing homes on true cost, travel time and access at once; a compare tray to put homes side by side |
| **Behavioural nudges** | "What to check before signing" next steps on every concern; asking about anything marked unknown |
| **Personalised recommendations** | Every check is personalised to the renter's budget, priorities and needs |
| **Data visualisation** | An interactive map of Edinburgh with views for budget, heating, step-free, lift, transport and travel time |
| **Accessibility** | Step-free entrance, lift and wheelchair route checks, personalised to the renter |

## Helping people **spend less** and **avoid costly mistakes**

- **See the real budget before signing.** A £50/month gap between the rent and the true cost is £600 a year.
- **Spot the high bill early.** The heating cost risk check flags homes where the EPC band suggests heavy energy costs.
- **Know if the rent is fair.** Rent context gives the renter something to negotiate with.
- **Avoid hidden repair and health costs.** Possible mould flagged from a photo becomes a question for the landlord, not a surprise in winter.
- **Save time.** No more piecing together council tax PDFs, energy price caps and route planners. It's all on one page.

## Built to be trusted with money decisions

- **Estimates are labelled as estimates**, with the source and date of every figure (council tax 2025/26 and 2026/27, the Ofgem cap for Oct to Dec 2026).
- **No false certainty.** Missing data is marked "unknown", and photo results are "possible issues to check", never a diagnosis.
- **Privacy first.** Photos screened by Gemini are processed in memory and never stored; uploads have location metadata removed; signed-in users can delete all their data in one step.

## Where to see it in the demo

1. Create a **tight-budget** profile.
2. On the map, switch between **Budget** and **Heating**: a cheap rent can still mean a high total.
3. Open a home: compare the **rent** with the **true monthly cost**, and look at the **rent context**.
4. Add **travel time** to your places to see the commute trade-off.
5. Screen a photo for **possible mould**, then open the report's **"what to check before signing"**.
